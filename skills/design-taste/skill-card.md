## Description:

Guides agents in creating, refining, and reviewing visual designs for interfaces, images, game art, and presentation materials.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[linkxzhou](https://clawhub.ai/user/linkxzhou)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

Designers and developers use this skill to plan, build, and critique visual interfaces and creative assets, including live-site and image reviews.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Reviewing a live website loads the supplied URL and may click common cookie or consent buttons during screenshot capture.

Mitigation: Use only URLs you are comfortable opening in your environment and review consent behavior before running a live-site review.
